#### Introduction

<a id="p-175"></a>
**175.** The general characteristics of the sphere of being have already been described (163. 171). We have seen, too, for what reasons the science of Logic necessarily begins with being (114 to 120). Nothing further need, therefore, be said under these heads. It only remains to say, by way of introduction, that the doctrine of being falls into three spheres of categories, viz. (1) quality, (2) quantity, and (3) measure. We proceed to deal with these in order.
