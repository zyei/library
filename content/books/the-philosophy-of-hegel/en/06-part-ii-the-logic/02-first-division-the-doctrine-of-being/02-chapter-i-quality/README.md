#### Chapter I. Quality

<a id="p-176"></a>
**176.** The specific category of *quality* is not the first in this sphere, but as all the categories in the sphere are, as we shall see, qualitative in character, the term quality is used as the name of the general sphere as well as of the special category. In the same way the term *being* is used in three significations. It is the name of the first category; it is the generic name of the three categories of the first triad; and it is the name of the entire sphere which includes quality, quantity, and measure.
