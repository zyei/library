###### B. Sum and Unity

<a id="p-222"></a>
**222.** A quantum is an aggregate, a many, a plurality. As such it is **sum**,—a sum of many ones. This is the moment of discretion. But as continuous these many ones form a **unity**—*one* quantum. These two moments, sum and unity, necessarily involve each other and can no more be separated than discrete and continuous magnitude.
