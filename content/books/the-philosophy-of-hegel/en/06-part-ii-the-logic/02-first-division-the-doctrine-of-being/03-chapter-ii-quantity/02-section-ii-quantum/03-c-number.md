###### C. Number

<a id="p-223"></a>
**223.** For the factors of sum and unity are precisely what make a number. To think the number seven, for example, we must first take an aggregate or *sum* of seven units, and we must then regard them as a *unity*, a *single* quantum. This gives us the **number**.

The category of number is thus the perfect expression of quantum. This may also be seen from the fact that it is by number that we *measure*, *i.e.* mete, set bounds or limits to, quantity. Suppose we have an *indefinite* number of men. This is a pure quantity. We then count them, and they become a *definite* quantity, a quantum. Thus number is the perfect expression of quantum.
