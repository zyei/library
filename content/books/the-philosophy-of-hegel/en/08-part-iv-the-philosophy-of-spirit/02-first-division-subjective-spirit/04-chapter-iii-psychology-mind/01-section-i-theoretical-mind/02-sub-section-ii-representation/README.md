###### Sub-Section II. Representation

<a id="p-501"></a>
**501.** Intuition is thus something both outward and inward. But the outwardness is the result of the activity of the mind itself. It is the mind which has itself outwardized the feeling. This outward is therefore *its own*, or in other words, it is an inward. The intuition thus freed from the external reference and made inward is **representation** (*Vorstellung*).[^iv-psych-3] Representation passes through the three phases of recollection, imagination, and memory.
